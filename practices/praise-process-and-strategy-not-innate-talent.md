# Praise process and strategy, not innate talent

**When to use**: When reviewing a teammate's work, mentoring, or evaluating engineering outcomes.

**Rule**: Reward concrete engineering behaviors—clear problem decomposition, thorough testing, sharing information, and learning from failures—rather than labeling people as smart or naturally gifted.

**Example**:
```text
Do: "Isolating that layout bug into a minimal repro before refactoring saved hours."
Do not: "You're a genius—you never make mistakes."
```

**Avoid**: Praising raw intelligence or glossing over errors as negatives, which discourages taking on hard tasks.

**Source**: [Developing a Growth Mindset with Carol Dweck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-71zdXCMU6A)
