# Optimize the top of the hiring funnel with fewer, tailored applications and authentic cover letters

**When to use**: When applying for engineering roles or helping mentees improve interview conversion rates.

**Rule**: Recognize that technical hiring is a steep funnel: companies receive hundreds of applications per role and filter most out before expensive engineer interviews. Rather than blasting 100 generic applications ("spray and pray"), invest the same hours into fewer, higher-signal applications. Write concise, genuine, company-specific cover letters that show real familiarity with the company's product/mission, connect your relevant projects directly to their stack or problems, and make it effortless for a busy reviewer to see why you belong in the next stage.

**Example**:
```text
High-signal cover letter structure:
1. Specific hook: why *this* team/product matters to you (from using it or reading their eng blog)
2. Proof of craft: 1-2 relevant projects/outcomes linked directly to their stack or domain
3. Clear close: enthusiasm for contributing and link to curated portfolio/demo
```

**Avoid**: Sending identical copy-pasted cover letters with swapped company names, optimizing purely for LeetCode while ignoring top-of-funnel application quality, or submitting hundreds of low-effort applications.
