# Adopt a growth mindset for debugging and learning

**When to use**: When hitting a confusing bug, unfamiliar language feature, or challenging exercise.

**Rule**: Treat coding and CSS fluency as skills developed through deliberate practice, strategy changes, and feedback—not innate talent. Stay with hard problems long enough to analyze errors and revise your mental model.

**Example**:
```text
Fixed framing: "I'm just bad at CSS."
Growth framing: "I haven't mapped how this property interacts with its container yet."
```

**Avoid**: Rushing past errors or avoiding hard tasks to protect an image of looking competent.

**Source**: [Introduction — CSS for JavaScript Developers](https://courses.joshwcomeau.com/css-for-js/00-introduction/01-welcome), [Developing a Growth Mindset with Carol Dweck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-71zdXCMU6A)
