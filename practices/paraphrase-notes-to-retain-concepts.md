# Paraphrase notes to retain concepts

**When to use**: When capturing takeaways from documentation, courses, or debugging sessions.

**Rule**: Write short notes that rephrase the rule in your own words and explain when it applies, rather than saving raw highlights or copying long passages.

**Example**:
```text
Bookmark note: "Use :first-of-type instead of :first-child when a heading precedes the first <p>."
(Not a full-page copy-paste.)
```

**Avoid**: Highlighting walls of text without synthesizing the actionable rule.

**Source**: [Notes — CSS for JavaScript Developers](https://courses.joshwcomeau.com/css-for-js/00-introduction/02.01-notes)
