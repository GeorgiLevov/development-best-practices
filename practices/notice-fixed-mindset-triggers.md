# Notice fixed-mindset triggers as cues

**When to use**: When facing a setback, critical code review, or a peer who outperforms you.

**Rule**: Watch for fixed-mindset reactions—defensiveness to feedback, hiding struggles, envy of skilled peers, or temptation to cut corners. Use those reactions as a cue to ask what you can learn or who can mentor you.

**Example**:
```text
Review comment points out a cleaner pattern:
1. Pause the urge to defend your first draft.
2. Ask why the alternative handles edge cases better and apply the insight.
```

**Avoid**: Hiding mistakes, blaming tools, or withholding knowledge to protect ego.

**Source**: [Developing a Growth Mindset with Carol Dweck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-71zdXCMU6A)
