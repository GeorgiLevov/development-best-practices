# Learn by active struggle and experimentation

**When to use**: When working through tutorials, docs, exercises, or unfamiliar APIs.

**Rule**: Do not consume technical material purely passively, and do not binge a course in the first burst of enthusiasm—watching is not the same as skill. Schedule a sustainable amount of practice, attempt exercises and challenges before checking solutions, and rebuild or tinker with working demos line by line so you form active mental connections. If you already know a section, still complete its challenges before skipping ahead. Pair active struggle with a **15–30 minute timebox**: if you are genuinely spinning without progress after ~20–30 minutes, study the reference solution until you understand *why* it works, then close it and rebuild the solution yourself.

**Example**:
```text
1. Read the problem requirements and draft a rough plan.
2. Build your own attempt in a live sandbox and observe what breaks.
3. Timebox stuck spots to 15-30 min; then study the solution, close it, and re-implement.
4. Finish a module’s challenges before skipping the rest of that section.
```

**Avoid**: Reading solutions or watching walkthroughs end-to-end without writing or modifying code, bingeing lessons without practice, or staying stuck on one detail for hours past diminishing returns.
