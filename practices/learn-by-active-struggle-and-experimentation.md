# Learn by active struggle and experimentation

**When to use**: When working through tutorials, docs, exercises, or unfamiliar APIs.

**Rule**: Do not consume technical material purely passively. Attempt exercises before checking solutions, and rebuild or tinker with working demos line by line so you form active mental connections. Pair active struggle with a **15–30 minute timebox**: if you are genuinely spinning without progress after ~20–30 minutes, study the reference solution until you understand *why* it works, then close it and rebuild the solution yourself.

**Example**:
```text
1. Read the problem requirements and draft a rough plan.
2. Build your own attempt in a live sandbox and observe what breaks.
3. Timebox stuck spots to 15-30 min; then study the solution, close it, and re-implement.
```

**Avoid**: Reading solutions or watching walkthroughs end-to-end without writing or modifying code, or staying stuck on one detail for hours past diminishing returns.
